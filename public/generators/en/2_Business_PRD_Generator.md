# 2_Business_PRD_Generator.md

**Goal:**
From a selected idea to a business-grounded plan (PRD = Product Requirements Document).
The generator uses everything from the Discovery phase and turns it into a concrete business plan with defined goal, value, market, ROI, and scope.

---

## **PHASE A - Context and problem**

> **P1:**
> "Briefly describe the idea or solution you want to elaborate."

> **P2:**
> "What problem or need does that solution address?"

> **P3:**
> "Who benefits the most - customers, internal team, the market?"

**AI note:**
Combine data from Discovery with the new context to determine market fit and baseline business value.

---

## **PHASE B - Solution and value**

> **P4:**
> "What is the main goal of the MVP (minimum viable solution)?"

> **P5:**
> "What is in scope and what is out of scope for the first version?"

> **P6:**
> "Which key features, automations, or processes would bring the biggest impact?"

**AI note:**
Estimate ROI, CAC, Payback, and Time-to-Value. Propose a table or chart showing expected impact of each component.

---

## **PHASE C - Plan and decision**

> **P7:**
> "What is the rough timeline and resources needed to launch the MVP?"

> **P8:**
> "How will success be measured (key metrics and expected results)?"

> **P9:**
> "Are there risks or conditions that could affect the decision to launch?"

**AI note:**
Compare results with previous phases, define business decision criteria, and generate a recommendation: Go / Refine / Hold.
